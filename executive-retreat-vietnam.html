SAMPLE ITINERARY

Day by day.

A sample five-day structure that can be rebuilt around your meeting agenda and team goals.

DAY 1

Arrive in Da Nang

Private airport welcome and group transfer to the hotel. Keep the first evening light with a welcome dinner and time to settle in.

DAY 2

Leadership sessions & hosted dinner

Morning and afternoon working sessions with breaks managed around the agenda. Finish with a private dinner designed for conversation rather than another formal meeting.

DAY 3

Work, then step outside

A shorter working block followed by a choice of golf, wellness, countryside or cultural experience. The day can split into parallel programmes if the group has different interests.

DAY 4

Hoi An & team time

Move the pace away from the meeting room with a private local experience, team lunch and time in Hoi An. Close the retreat with a memorable final dinner.

DAY 5

Departure

Breakfast at leisure, optional final check-in session and private transfers timed to each departure flight.
