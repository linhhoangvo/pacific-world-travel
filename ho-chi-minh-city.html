Why visit Ho Chi Minh City

Vietnam’s commercial capital is also one of its most flexible travel bases.

Ho Chi Minh City is the natural hub for business in southern Vietnam, with strong hotel, dining and transport infrastructure.

Outside the working schedule, the city offers food, architecture, neighbourhoods and nightlife that can be shaped around the traveller rather than a fixed sightseeing programme.

It is also the gateway to the Mekong Delta, Phu Quoc and southern golf destinations.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions about Ho Chi Minh City

How many days do you need in Ho Chi Minh City?

Two to four nights works well for most business or private stays, depending on meetings and onward travel.

Is Ho Chi Minh City good for business travel?

Yes. It is Vietnam’s main commercial centre, with extensive international-standard hotels, meeting facilities and private transport options.

Can you add golf to a business trip?

Yes. Golf days can be arranged around meetings and client programmes with private transfers.

Where can you go after Ho Chi Minh City?

The Mekong Delta and Phu Quoc are two of the most natural southern extensions.
