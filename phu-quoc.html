Why visit Phu Quoc

A resort island that works equally well for downtime, golf and incentives.

Phu Quoc offers a broad range of premium resorts, beaches and private experiences, making it a natural final stop after a busier Vietnam itinerary.

The island is also useful for executive retreats and incentives because the programme can combine structured time with genuine resort downtime.

Direct air access and golf options make it easy to shape the stay around different traveller profiles.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions about Phu Quoc

How many nights should you stay in Phu Quoc?

Three to five nights works well for most resort stays and gives enough time for the island to feel genuinely relaxing.

Is Phu Quoc good for golf?

Yes. Golf can be integrated into a resort stay without taking over the entire island programme.

Can Phu Quoc be combined with Ho Chi Minh City?

Yes. The city and island make a natural southern pairing, particularly at the end of a longer Vietnam journey.

Is Phu Quoc suitable for incentive groups?

Yes. Resorts, private boats, dining, golf and wellness experiences can all be combined for tailored group programmes.
