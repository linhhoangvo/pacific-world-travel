Good to know

Frequently asked questions about incentives & corporate events

What is an incentive travel programme?

An incentive journey is a company-sponsored travel experience designed to recognise performance, reward teams, strengthen relationships or motivate future achievement.

Can you manage both the event and the travel logistics?

Yes. We can coordinate hotels, venues, airport arrivals, group transfers, dining, local experiences and on-site support as one programme.

Can incentive programmes include golf or wellness?

Yes. Golf, wellness, local culture, food and private experiences can all be integrated around the profile of the group.

Can you manage private dinners or celebration events?

Yes. We can support venue sourcing, guest movement, hospitality planning and local coordination for private dinners, celebrations and corporate social events.
