Good to know

Frequently asked questions about leadership retreats

How long should a leadership retreat be?

Two to four days often works well, depending on the amount of strategy time, travel required and how much informal team time you want to include.

Can you arrange private meeting spaces at a resort?

Yes. We can source properties with suitable private meeting rooms, breakout areas and hospitality spaces for senior teams.

Can the retreat include golf or wellness?

Yes. Golf, spa, wellness, private dining and local experiences can be added selectively around the core leadership agenda.

Can you support confidential or senior-level groups?

Yes. We can shape transport, venue and hospitality arrangements around privacy, discretion and a smaller number of senior participants.
