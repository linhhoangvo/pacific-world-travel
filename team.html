Our Team

An experienced local team bringing destination knowledge, trusted relationships and hands-on support to every business, golf and corporate journey.

Local expertise.
Personal service.

Our team brings together experience across operations, sales, product development and guiding in Vietnam. We combine professional travel management with deep local knowledge, careful coordination and a personal approach to make every journey feel seamless from planning to arrival.

01

Rosie Hoa Duong

Founder & Managing Director

Hoa brings more than a decade of experience in tourism and travel, together with a Master’s degree in International Sustainable Tourism Management from Monash University. She leads the company with a focus on thoughtful journeys, strong local relationships and travel that connects guests more deeply with Vietnam.

02

Linh Vo

Operations Manager

Originally from Quang Binh and based in Hoi An for many years, Linh manages the details behind each journey. Her focus is seamless coordination, reliable support and making sure travellers can move through Vietnam comfortably and confidently.

03

Hanh Nguyen

Sales Manager

Hanh works closely with guests and partners from the first conversation onward. She listens carefully to travel goals, turns ideas into practical itineraries and helps ensure every proposal feels personal, thoughtful and well supported from start to finish.

04

Van Nguyen

Product Development Manager

Van develops tours and destination experiences around the details that make Vietnam memorable — local stories, family recipes, quiet village roads, beautiful landscapes and genuine encounters. Her approach keeps every experience natural, meaningful and rooted in place.

05

Kamala Kieu Dung

Tour Guide

Kamala brings together guiding, travel and a strong interest in yoga and mindful living. She enjoys introducing guests to Vietnam through local food, everyday stories, hidden corners, nature and the small human moments that make a journey feel authentic.

06

Vinh Bui

Tour Guide

Born and raised in Ho Chi Minh City, Vinh has worked in tourism and hospitality since 2016. He loves sharing Vietnam through local stories, authentic food, meaningful encounters and the everyday experiences travellers may not discover on their own.

Local knowledge is not an add-on. It is the foundation of how we plan, solve problems and make every journey feel effortless.
