Good to know

Frequently asked questions about MICE in Vietnam

What does MICE stand for?

MICE stands for Meetings, Incentives, Conferences and Exhibitions — business events and corporate travel programmes designed around collaboration, learning, recognition or industry engagement.

Can you manage venue, hotel and delegate logistics together?

Yes. We can coordinate venue sourcing, accommodation, airport arrivals, transfers, rooming requirements and on-site guest movements as one programme.

Can a conference include incentive or destination experiences?

Yes. Dining, golf, team activities and local experiences can be built around the core agenda so they add value without making the programme feel over-scheduled.

Can you support exhibitions or hybrid event requirements?

We can coordinate local venue, delegate and supplier requirements for exhibitions and hybrid programmes, with the exact technical scope shaped around the event brief.
