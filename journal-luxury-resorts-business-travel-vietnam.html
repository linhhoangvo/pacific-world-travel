Where to Stay: Luxury Resorts for Business Travel in Vietnam

The right hotel should support the working day as well as the hours around it. These are the details we look for when planning an executive stay.

Da Nang and Hoi An

For leadership retreats and small executive groups, the Da Nang–Hoi An coast makes it possible to combine private meeting time, wellness and golf. Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai offers a tranquil setting near Hoi An, while InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort provides a more secluded peninsula experience. Meeting facilities and group arrangements should be confirmed for each event.

Ho Chi Minh City

For a city-focused business trip, proximity to the meeting venue often matters more than a resort-style setting. Choose a well-connected premium hotel with reliable meeting space, suitable room categories and a transfer plan that accommodates traffic. A separate coastal retreat may suit the final night of a longer executive itinerary.

What business travellers actually need

Check Wi-Fi and workspace suitability, breakfast hours, early-arrival options, a quiet room location and the ability to coordinate several arrivals. For a corporate group, assess private dining options, meeting equipment, rooming lists and payment procedures before selecting the property.

Building in downtime

A resort works particularly well when recovery is part of the brief: an unhurried breakfast, a spa appointment or a private dinner can complement a focused morning session. Keep free time genuinely free rather than scheduling every hour.
