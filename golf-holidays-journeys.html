Where to play

Choose the golf region for the journey you want.

Vietnam’s golf regions feel very different. Course style, transfer time, weather, resorts and what you can do between rounds all shape the right choice.

Da Nang & Hoi An · Central Vietnam

Coastal golf with the easiest mix of courses, resorts and evenings out.

A strong first choice for private golf holidays and mixed groups: play in the morning, return to the beach or Hoi An afterwards, and keep transfers straightforward.

Hoiana Shores · Ba Na Hills · other Central Vietnam courses by request
Lăng Cô & Hue · Central Coast

A quieter golf stay between mountains, sea and heritage.

Best when the golf is part of a slower resort stay, executive retreat or a longer route connecting Da Nang and Hue.

Laguna Golf Lăng Cô · resort stays · Hue extension
Ho Chi Minh City · Southern Vietnam

City energy with golf that works around business or a longer Vietnam itinerary.

Useful for travellers who want to combine meetings, dining and city time with one or more rounds before moving elsewhere in the country.

City golf · private transport · business-trip extensions
Cam Ranh & Nha Trang · South Central Coast

Golf and resort time with less movement between rounds.

A good fit for travellers who want a simpler stay-and-play rhythm: resort, golf, beach and enough downtime to make the trip feel like a holiday.

Cam Ranh golf · beach resorts · stay & play
