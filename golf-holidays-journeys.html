Vietnam golf at a glance

NorthHanoi & Ha LongGenerally strongest from October to April, with capital-city golf and easy extensions into northern Vietnam.
CentralDa Nang · Hoi An · HueGenerally strongest from February to August, with one of Vietnam’s best combinations of coastal golf, resorts and culture.
SouthHo Chi Minh City & beyondPlayable year-round, making the south easy to combine with business travel, city stays or a coastal extension.
Typical arrangementsOne itinerary, one local teamHotels, green fees, caddies, carts, private transfers, daily breakfast and on-ground support can all be coordinated together.

Seasonality is indicative and can vary by destination, rainfall and individual course conditions.
