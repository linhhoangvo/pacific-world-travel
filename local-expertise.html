Vietnam, region by region

The country does not operate as one destination.

Each region has its own rhythm, transport patterns, strengths and constraints. We plan around those differences rather than forcing the same programme everywhere.

North

Hanoi · Ninh Binh · Ha Long · Sapa

Works well for Business stays, heritage, cruise extensions and multi-stop private journeys.

What matters locally Road-transfer sequencing, overnight choices and how much can realistically fit into one day.

Central

Hue · Da Nang · Hoi An

Works well for MICE, golf, heritage, resort stays and corporate extensions.

What matters locally Choosing the right base, airport access and balancing coast, city and heritage without over-moving the group.

South

Ho Chi Minh City · Mekong Delta

Works well for Business travel, events, dining, client hosting and short regional extensions.

What matters locally City traffic, hotel location, meeting schedules and keeping day trips realistic around business commitments.

Coast & islands

Cam Ranh · Nha Trang · Phu Quoc

Works well for Incentives, retreats, golf, wellness and post-business downtime.

What matters locally Resort location, airport proximity and whether the programme should stay contained or include outside experiences.

Local decisions that change the trip

The details are operational, not decorative.

Arrival

Where exactly is the guest met?

Terminal procedure, signage, baggage timing and driver coordination affect the first 30 minutes more than the itinerary does.

Hotel

Is the location right for the schedule?

The best hotel on paper may be the wrong hotel if every meeting requires crossing the city.

Golf

Does the tee time fit the rest of the day?

Course choice, transfer time, player level and post-round plans should be considered together.

Events

Can guests move through the venue easily?

Arrival flow, coach access, private spaces and nearby accommodation matter as much as how the venue photographs.
