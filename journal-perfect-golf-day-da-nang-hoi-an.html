A Perfect Golf Day in Da Nang & Hoi An

An early tee time, a long lunch and a gentle evening in Hoi An: a simple day that makes the most of central Vietnam.

7:00 am — Head for the fairways

Arrange a private pickup from your hotel and set off with time for check-in and warm-up. Depending on the day's routing and available tee times, choose a coastal round near Hoi An or a course closer to the hills outside Da Nang. Morning play can be more comfortable in warmer months.

12:30 pm — Lunch without rushing

After the round, head back towards the coast for Vietnamese seafood or a relaxed restaurant lunch. A pre-arranged table is helpful for groups, especially when everyone wants to dine together.

3:00 pm — Rest, spa or the beach

Leave a few hours open. Return to the resort for a spa treatment, enjoy the beach or spend some quiet time by the pool. Golf trips are better when the schedule allows room to unwind.

6:30 pm — An evening in Hoi An

Finish with an easy walk through Hoi An's old town and a private dinner. For corporate guests, this can be a memorable setting for informal conversation after a day on the course. We can coordinate transport, tee times, dining and the wider itinerary around your group's pace.
