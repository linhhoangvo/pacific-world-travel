Six Golf Courses Worth Travelling For in Vietnam

From coastal links-style layouts to mountain backdrops, these courses offer very different reasons to build a Vietnam golf journey.

Central coast favourites

Hoiana Shores Golf Club near Hoi An is a natural choice for golfers who enjoy firm, open coastal terrain and sea breezes. Pair a morning round with the old town, a short drive away. Ba Na Hills Golf Club, inland from Da Nang, has a different atmosphere: foothill scenery and a layout that suits travellers keen to experience more than one style of course.

Northern Vietnam

BRG Legend Hill Golf Resort near Hanoi is known for its dual-green concept, giving returning players a different perspective. It is a practical addition to a Hanoi-based trip with meetings or sightseeing. Further afield, Chi Linh Golf Club offers a green, undulating setting for groups willing to travel outside the capital.

Southern choices

Around Ho Chi Minh City, golf days can be paired with client meetings, city dining and onward travel. For a coastal escape, The Bluffs Grand Ho Tram is particularly appealing to experienced golfers who want dramatic terrain and a stay outside the city.

Choosing the right course

A good choice depends on playing ability, preferred scenery, available travel time and whether the round is part of a wider company itinerary. Confirm current visitor access, tee-time availability, caddie arrangements and course policies before committing to flights or a group programme.
