A few courses we might shortlist

Choose the course for the guest, the schedule and the occasion.

Vietnam has very different golf experiences even within one region. We shortlist the course around transfer time, playing style, scenery, privacy and what you want the day to feel like.

Hoi An · Central Vietnam

Hoiana Shores Golf Club

A coastal Robert Trent Jones II layout through natural dunes, close enough to Hoi An to combine golf with a polished dinner, resort stay or evening in the old town.

Corporate fit: client hosting, premium leisure extension and groups who want the golf to connect naturally with Hoi An.
Da Nang · Central Vietnam

Ba Na Hills Golf Club

A Luke Donald-designed course in the foothills west of Da Nang, with a mountain setting that feels distinctly different from the coast.

Corporate fit: an easy full-day golf option from Da Nang when the group wants a strong course without adding an overnight move.
Lang Co · Between Da Nang & Hue

Laguna Golf Lăng Cô

A Sir Nick Faldo-designed course between the mountains and the sea, with varied terrain and a resort setting that works particularly well when golf is part of a longer hosted programme.

Corporate fit: executive retreats, overnight hosting and programmes connecting Da Nang, Lăng Cô and Hue.
