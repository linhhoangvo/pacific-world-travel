Planning a Golf Trip to Vietnam: Seasons, Packing & Tee Times

A practical guide to weather, course etiquette, golf equipment and the small decisions that make a trip easier.

When is the best time to play?

Conditions vary significantly across Vietnam. In the north, spring and autumn often offer more comfortable temperatures than peak summer; winter can be cooler. Central Vietnam generally has a wetter period later in the year, while the south commonly has a drier season from roughly December through April. Weather can change, so check the forecast for your exact destination and dates.

What to wear

Most courses expect collared golf shirts and appropriate golf trousers, shorts or skirts, together with suitable golf shoes. Denim, beachwear and metal spikes may be restricted. Individual clubs set their own policies, so check the dress code before travelling.

Travelling with golf clubs

Check your airline's sports-equipment rules, weight allowances, protective packing requirements and any additional charges before departure. For multi-city journeys, make sure the transfer vehicle can accommodate hard travel cases along with regular suitcases.

Tee times and on-course arrangements

Book popular courses ahead for weekends and group travel. Confirm caddie and cart arrangements, rental-club availability, handicaps if relevant, and cancellation terms. Leave room for transfer times and weather changes rather than planning a tight connection straight after a round.

Make it a complete journey

Combining golf with resort stays, good restaurants and local experiences turns separate rounds into a memorable holiday or executive itinerary. A local partner can help coordinate the moving parts.
