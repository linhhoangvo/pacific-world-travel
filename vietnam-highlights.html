JOURNEY OVERVIEW

Vietnam, connected beautifully.

A ten-day introduction to Vietnam designed around the places first-time and returning travellers most often want to experience. Begin in Hanoi, move through the limestone landscapes of Ha Long Bay, slow the pace in Hoi An, then finish in the energy of Ho Chi Minh City.

This is a starting point rather than a fixed package. Hotels, pacing, dining, guides, activities and private transport can all be tailored around your travellers and preferred style.
