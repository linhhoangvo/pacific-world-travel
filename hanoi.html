Why visit Hanoi

A capital with enough depth to feel different every day.

Hanoi brings together history, contemporary life and a strong sense of place in a compact city that works equally well for business stays and private travel.

Its real appeal often sits between the headline sights — in food, neighbourhoods, cafés, lakes and the slower moments that make the city feel lived-in rather than staged.

It is also the most natural gateway to Ha Long Bay, Ninh Binh and Sapa, making it easy to shape a wider northern Vietnam journey from one base.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions about Hanoi

How many days do you need in Hanoi?

Two or three days works well for a first stay, while a longer visit leaves more room for food, neighbourhoods, museums and onward excursions.

Is Hanoi good for business travel?

Yes. The city has a broad range of international-standard hotels, meeting facilities and private transport options, while still offering distinctive local experiences around the working schedule.

What can Hanoi be paired with?

Ha Long Bay, Ninh Binh and Sapa are natural additions, depending on whether you want coast, countryside or mountains.

What is the best way to get around Hanoi?

For a tailored programme, private car and driver is the easiest option for airport transfers and scheduled movements, with walking used selectively in the historic centre.
