Hanoi

Vietnam’s capital is a practical base for business and a rewarding place to spend time after the meetings are done. We manage executive travel, client programmes, golf days and onward journeys across Northern Vietnam from one local plan.

WHY HANOI

A strong business base.
A city worth staying for.

For a short executive visit, Hanoi is straightforward to organise: international air access, a wide choice of business hotels and reliable private transport across the city.

When the schedule opens up, the city gives you plenty to work with — a client dinner in the Old Quarter, a round of golf, or an easy extension to Ha Long Bay or Ninh Binh without rebuilding the trip from scratch.

HANOI FAQS

The practical questions.

How many nights should a business traveller spend in Hanoi?

Two to four nights is a practical starting point for a focused programme. We build around meeting times, flight schedules and any golf or leisure extension you want to add.

Can Pacific World manage airport transfers and executive transport?

Yes. We coordinate airport transfers, private vehicles, drivers and local logistics as part of the wider travel programme.

Can a Hanoi business trip include golf?

Yes. Golf can work as client entertainment, a free-day activity or the start of a longer golf journey, with the logistics planned around the business schedule.

Can Hanoi be combined with Ha Long Bay or Ninh Binh?

Yes. Both work naturally as short extensions from Hanoi and can be managed as part of the same itinerary.

Do you arrange programmes for corporate groups?

Yes. We manage executive groups, client programmes, incentives and corporate teams, including hotels, vehicles, dining, golf and local experiences.

Planning Hanoi?

Send us the dates, who is travelling and what needs to happen on the ground. We’ll build the trip around it.
