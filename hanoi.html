HANOI FAQS

Planning the details.

How many nights should a business traveller spend in Hanoi?

For a focused programme, two to four nights is a practical starting point. We can build the schedule around meeting times, flight patterns and any golf or leisure extension you want to add.

Can Pacific World manage executive airport transfers and local transport?

Yes. We can coordinate airport transfers, private vehicles, drivers and on-the-ground logistics as part of the wider travel programme.

Can a Hanoi business trip include golf?

Yes. Golf can be added as a client-hosting day, a leisure extension or part of a wider golf journey, with tee-time and transport logistics coordinated around the business schedule.

Can Hanoi be combined with Ha Long Bay or Ninh Binh?

Yes. Hanoi works well as the hub for a northern Vietnam extension, allowing the city stay and onward travel to be managed as one itinerary.

Do you arrange programmes for groups and corporate teams?

Yes. Pacific World can shape Hanoi programmes for executives, client groups, incentives and corporate teams, including stays, vehicles, dining, golf and tailored local experiences.
