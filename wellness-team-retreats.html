Good to know

Frequently asked questions about wellness & team retreats

Does a wellness retreat have to include yoga or spa?

No. Wellness can mean rest, nature, better food, more movement, stronger team connection or simply a healthier pace. We shape the programme around the group.

Can the retreat include working sessions?

Yes. Light meeting or strategy sessions can be included, with enough space around them to preserve the retreat atmosphere.

How long should a team retreat be?

Two to four nights often works well, depending on travel time, group size and how much structured activity you want to include.

Can you tailor activities for different fitness levels?

Yes. We can select optional or low-impact activities and build flexibility into the programme so participants can engage comfortably.
