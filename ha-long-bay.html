Ha Long Bay

An iconic seascape best experienced slowly — with time on the water, a comfortable overnight cruise and a seamless connection from Hanoi.

Why visit

A landscape that changes the pace of the journey.

Ha Long Bay works beautifully as a short extension after Hanoi — close enough to connect easily, yet different enough to feel like a complete change of scene.

For private travellers, clients or small groups, the right cruise can turn a simple overnight stay into one of the most memorable moments of a Vietnam itinerary.

01Stay overnight

One night is enough for a first experience; two gives the bay more room to breathe.

02Travel privately

Private transfers from Hanoi keep the journey direct, comfortable and easy to coordinate.

03Choose the cruise well

The vessel, route and pace matter more here than packing the itinerary with activities.

The experience

Less rushing. More time on the water.

Ha Long Bay is at its best when the programme leaves space for the landscape itself. A relaxed cruise, a quiet evening on deck and an early morning among the limestone islands can be more memorable than a long checklist of stops.

Kayaking, caves, floating villages and seafood dining can all be added, but the strongest itineraries keep the rhythm gentle.
