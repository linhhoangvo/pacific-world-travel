Why visit Ha Long Bay

One of Vietnam’s most iconic landscapes, best enjoyed without rushing.

Ha Long Bay is one of the most recognisable landscapes in Vietnam — limestone formations rising from calm emerald water, changing character with the light and weather throughout the day.

While many travellers visit on a day trip from Hanoi, the experience becomes far more memorable with time to stay longer. An overnight cruise allows space for sunset on deck, a quieter morning on the water and a more relaxed rhythm overall.

It also works beautifully as part of a wider northern Vietnam journey, pairing naturally with Hanoi before the bay or Ninh Binh afterwards for a route that feels connected, calm and well paced.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions about Ha Long Bay

Why is Ha Long Bay famous?

Ha Long Bay is known for its dramatic limestone karsts, emerald water and one of the most distinctive seascapes in Vietnam. The landscape is especially atmospheric from the water, which is why overnight cruising is such a natural way to experience it.

Is Ha Long Bay a day trip from Hanoi?

Yes, but we generally recommend staying overnight. More time on the bay creates a calmer experience and lets you enjoy the landscape beyond the busiest daytime hours.

Is Ha Long Bay too touristy?

Some areas can be busy, particularly during peak daytime periods. Choosing the right vessel, route and timing can make the experience feel much more private and considered.

Can you swim in Ha Long Bay?

Depending on the cruise route, weather and local conditions, swimming may be possible in selected areas. Kayaking, cave visits and quiet time on deck are also common ways to experience the bay.
